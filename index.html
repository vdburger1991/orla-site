What Orla does

30+ symptoms, the way you actually experience them

Hot flashes, night sweats, sleep trouble, mood changes, brain fog, joint pain, palpitations, vaginal and urinary changes, libido, skin and hair changes, and more. Record how strong each one is and when it happened — in a few taps, even at 2am.

A real record of your HRT, not a daily checkbox

Log hormone therapy properly: type, dose and route — patch, gel, spray, tablet and more — plus supplements. When your dose or preparation changes, Orla keeps the previous one on a dated timeline instead of overwriting it. That history is exactly what a doctor asks about.

Cycle logging that doesn't assume you're still cycling

Periods in the transition get irregular, skip, or stop — and plenty of people aren't cycling at all. Orla logs whatever is actually happening, without forcing a "cycle" frame on you. Having no cycle to log is a first-class experience here, not a broken one.

Apple Health alongside your own notes

With your permission, Orla can read wrist temperature, heart rate variability, resting heart rate and sleep from Apple Health, and show them next to what you've logged. Read-only — Orla never writes anything back to Health.

A doctor-ready PDF, on demand

Pick a date range and Orla builds a clean summary: how often each symptom showed up, your HRT timeline, and your cycle notes — laid out to be readable in a short appointment. Bring it, print it, or email it ahead.

A plain-language library

Twenty short, readable articles on what happens during the transition and the ways hormone therapy is commonly taken — written in plain English, so you walk in informed.

Private by design

Your health record is yours. Orla carries no ads and no advertising SDKs, and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs touch your health entries. Your data is never sold.

Your logs stay on your device, with optional iCloud sync that you control if you want your record backed up and available across your own devices.
