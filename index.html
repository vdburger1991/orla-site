Before you tap.

Is it really free for seven days?

Yes — seven days free on either plan. It renews afterwards unless you cancel first.

How do I cancel?

In your Apple ID settings, under Subscriptions, any time before the seven days are up.

What does it cost after that?

A monthly plan or a yearly one. The App Store shows the price for your country before you confirm anything.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet. Orla is iPhone only, and needs iOS 17 or later.
